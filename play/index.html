GAME MENU
=========
DUNGEON SHOOTER
A fast-paced roguelike shooter
Navigate procedurally generated dungeons
Fight enemies and find better weapons

MINESWEEPER
Classic puzzle game
16x16 grid with 40 mines
Click numbers to clear adjacent cells
Flag all the mines to win!
SOLITAIRE
The game you play in a waiting room

BUBBLE SHOOTER
Match 3+ bubbles to pop them

Classic arcade space shooter

Survive as long as you can!
SPACE INVADERS
Classic arcade alien invasion game
Destroy waves of invaders before they land
Hide behind shields for protection
Each level brings more invaders!
