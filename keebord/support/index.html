Keebord support

Email pez@pez.sh with what you tried, what happened, and your iPhone model and iOS version. I read everything.

Common questions

How do I turn Keebord on?
Open Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard... and choose Keebord. Then, in any text field, hold the globe key and pick Keebord. The Keebord app has a field to try it in.

Does Keebord need Full Access?
No, and it never asks for it. Everything works without it, which is also why nothing you type can leave your iPhone.

How do I type in Danish or Vietnamese?
Turn the languages on in the Keebord app, under Languages. A language key appears next to the emoji key: tap it to switch, or hold it to pick.

How does Vietnamese input work?
Telex: aa = â, aw = ă, ee = ê, oo = ô, ow = ơ, uw = ư, dd = đ, and s f r x j add the tones (z removes one). Type a key twice to undo it. You can also swipe the letters without accents and Keebord picks the accented word; other tones show above the keys.

Auto-correct changed a word I meant.
Press backspace right after the change and the word comes back. Keebord won't correct that word again. You can turn auto-correct off in the Keebord app.

Keebord doesn't know a word.
Type it twice and it becomes swipeable and shows up in suggestions.

How do I change the theme?
In the Keebord app, under Theme. Every theme has a light and a dark look and follows your iPhone. The keyboard picks up the change the next time it opens.

Why does my iPhone switch to its own keyboard sometimes?
iOS always uses its own keyboard for password fields and a few other secure fields. That's by design and can't be changed by any keyboard.
