Keebord: a fast swipe keyboard for iPhone

Keebord is a keyboard built for speed. Swipe across the letters or tap them out, and it keeps up.

- Swipe to type: draw a word across the keys; it learns the words you use
- Suggestions: completions, the next word, and an emoji when it fits
- Auto-correct you can turn off, undone by a backspace right after
- A period key between space and return, with ? ! , ' and more on a hold
- English, Danish and Vietnamese: æ ø å keys, Telex, and swiping plain letters to accented words
- Seven themes, each with a light and a dark look
- Private: no Full Access, no network, nothing you type leaves your iPhone
