Keebord privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026.

Keebord doesn't collect any personal data. I don't run any servers for it, and there's no account, analytics, advertising or tracking.

The keyboard has no Full Access
Keebord doesn't ask for Full Access, so iOS doesn't let the keyboard connect to the internet or share data with other apps. What you type goes only to the app you're typing in.

What's stored, and where
- To improve swiping and suggestions, the keyboard keeps a count of the words you use and which words follow each other. It's stored on your iPhone, inside the keyboard's own storage, and never leaves it.
- Keebord doesn't learn in password, email, URL, number or one-time-code fields.
- Your settings (theme, languages and so on) are stored on your iPhone.
- Deleting Keebord deletes all of it.
- The developer has no access to any of this data.

Crash reports
If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports. These don't contain anything you've typed.

Children
Keebord isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
