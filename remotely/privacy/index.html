Remotely privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026.

Remotely doesn't collect any personal data. I don't run any servers for it, and there's no account, analytics, advertising or tracking.

What's stored, and where
- The connections you save (names, addresses, usernames and settings) are stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account.
- Passwords and private keys are stored in iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end. I can't see them.
- The developer has no access to any of this data.

Your connections
Remotely connects directly from your device to the servers you set up. Nothing passes through servers of mine.

Purchases
In-app purchases are handled by Apple. I don't receive your payment details.

Crash reports
If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports. These don't contain your connections, passwords or keys.

Children
Remotely isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
