Remotely: SSH, VNC and RDP for iPhone Duo, iPhone and iPad

Remotely connects you to your servers and desktops, and it's built around iPhone Duo's hinge:

- Closed: browse and start connections on the outer display
- Half folded: the session above the fold, keyboard and trackpad below it
- Fully open: the whole inner display for your terminal or desktop

- SSH with Ed25519 and RSA keys, key generation and host key pinning
- RDP (Windows and xrdp) and VNC, with touchscreen and trackpad modes
- A keyboard made for the command line: Esc, Ctrl, Alt, F-keys, arrows
- Connections, settings and keys synced with iCloud and iCloud Keychain
- No account, no tracking, no data collected

Free for up to three connections. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited connections on all your devices.
