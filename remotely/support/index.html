Remotely support

Email pez@pez.sh with what you tried, what happened, and your device and iOS version. I read everything.

Common questions

How many connections can I save for free?
Three. "Remotely Unlimited" is a one-time purchase that removes the limit on every device signed in to your Apple Account. Connections you already have always keep working.

I bought Remotely Unlimited but it isn't unlocked.
Open Settings in Remotely and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

My connections or keys don't appear on my other device.
Remotely syncs with iCloud. Check that iCloud and iCloud Keychain are on in the Settings app on both devices, and give it a minute with Remotely open.

Which SSH keys work?
Ed25519 and RSA private keys in OpenSSH format (e.g. ~/.ssh/id_ed25519), including passphrase-protected ones, and unencrypted PEM RSA keys. Add keys in Settings > SSH Keys, or generate one there and copy its public key into ~/.ssh/authorized_keys on your server.

"Host key changed" when connecting over SSH.
The server presented a different key than the first time. That's expected if the server was reinstalled; otherwise check with whoever runs it before trusting the new key.

Connecting to a Mac over VNC.
In macOS System Settings > General > Sharing > Screen Sharing, turn on "VNC viewers may control screen with password" and set a password.

Connecting over RDP.
Windows needs Remote Desktop turned on (Pro, Enterprise or Server editions). Linux works with xrdp.

Remotely asks for Local Network access.
iOS asks before an app connects to devices on your network. Allow it to reach servers at home or in the office.
